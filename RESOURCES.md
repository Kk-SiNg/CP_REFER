# Competitive Programming Resources

## Knowledge

### Core Algorithm References
- [CP-Algorithms (e-maxx eng)](https://cp-algorithms.com/)
  The gold standard for CP algorithm explanations with implementations. Use for: any algorithm or data structure explanation with proven C++ code.

- [USACO Guide](https://usaco.guide/)
  Structured curriculum from Bronze to Platinum with curated problem lists. Use for: topic-ordered practice and learning paths.

- [Codeforces EDU](https://codeforces.com/edu/courses)
  Official Codeforces educational courses (Binary Search, Segment Trees, DSU, etc.). Use for: interactive step-by-step learning with built-in judge.

- [Colin Galen's YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ColinGalen)
  High-quality competitive programming tutorials from a top-rated competitor. Use for: DP, graph theory, segment trees, and contest strategy.

- [Errichto's YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Errichto)
  Contest walkthroughs and technique deep-dives. Use for: thinking process, editorial-style explanations, and advanced topics.

- [Competitive Programmer's Handbook by Antti Laaksonen](https://cses.fi/book/book.pdf)
  Free PDF covering most topics needed up to ~2000 rating. Use for: concise theory with examples, great as a reference book.

### Practice Problem Sets
- [CSES Problem Set](https://cses.fi/problemset/)
  ~300 carefully curated problems covering all standard CP topics. Use for: systematic topic-by-topic practice.

- [A2OJ Ladders (archived)](https://earthshakira.github.io/a2oj-clientside/server/Ladders.html)
  Rating-sorted CF problems. Use for: progressive difficulty practice at your exact level.

- [Codeforces Problemset (filtered by rating & tags)](https://codeforces.com/problemset)
  Use for: targeted practice on specific ratings (1300-1600) and weak tags (DP, graphs, data structures).

### Contest Strategy
- [How to practice Competitive Programming by Um_nik](https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/98806)
  Advice from a legendary competitor on practice methodology. Use for: structuring practice sessions.

- [Self-deception: maybe why you're still grey by Radewoosh](https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/98621)
  Honest take on why people plateau. Use for: mindset and avoiding common practice traps.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Codeforces Blogs](https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/13529)
  Active blog community with editorials, tutorials, and discussions. The best place to ask questions about specific problems.

- [r/CompetitiveProgramming](https://reddit.com/r/CompetitiveProgramming)
  Subreddit with weekly discussions, resource recommendations, and rating improvement threads.

- [CF Discord Servers](https://discord.gg/competitive-programming)
  Real-time discussion with other competitors. Good for: finding virtual contest partners and getting unstuck.

## Gaps
- No strong resource found for "consistency systems" specific to CP — most advice is generic productivity advice applied to CP. Will need to develop a custom consistency framework.
