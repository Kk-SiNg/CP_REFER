You've earned Pupil. The exams are over. This is the window.

This lesson lays out a structured 8-week roadmap that maps your CF weak spots to specific CSES sections and 1400-rated CF problems. Every problem on this list was chosen because it targets the exact skill gap between your current rating and Specialist.

The Core Idea

CSES builds the technique. CF 1400s test whether you can apply it under contest pressure. You alternate between the two: learn a pattern on CSES, then prove you own it on CF.

The 1300→1400 Wall

You've solved 104 problems at rating 1300, but only 15 at 1400. That's a 7× dropoff. The problems at 1400 aren't harder in a general sense — they require specific algorithmic patterns (DP transitions, graph modeling, clever binary search) that greedy intuition alone can't crack.

Daily Routine (3 hrs)

The Post-Exams Daily Block

0:00–0:30 Warm-up: 1× CF problem at 1200-1300 (speed training — solve in under 15 min) 0:30–1:30 CSES grind: 1-2 CSES problems from current phase (learn the pattern, don't rush) 1:30–2:30 CF 1400 practice: 1× problem at 1400. Struggle for 40 min, then read editorial if stuck. 2:30–3:00 Review: Write down what you learned. If you read an editorial, re-implement from scratch.

Rules of Engagement

  • Never skip the reimplementation. If you read an editorial, close it, and code the solution from memory. This is where storage strength is built.
  • CSES before CF each day. CSES teaches the pattern. CF tests if you've internalized it. Order matters.
  • Don't skip contests. Rate every Div2/Div3 you can. Rating only moves when you compete. Aim for 1-2 contests per week minimum.
  • Track what stumped you. After each session, note the problem and why you got stuck. Revisit those problems 1 week later.
  • No problems below 1200. You've outgrown them. Every minute on an 800-rated problem is a minute not spent in your zone of proximal development.
