Daily Practice Checklist

Print this or keep it open. Follow it every day. No decisions needed — just execute.

Session Structure (2 hours)

10 min
Warmup. Solve 1 problem at 1200-1300. Pick from CF problemset. Prioritize speed. Don't overthink.
60 min
Push. Attempt 1-2 problems at 1400-1500. CF problemset, 1400-1500. Struggle is the point. If stuck for 30+ min on one, switch to the second.
30 min
Upsolve. For problems you couldn't crack: read the editorial, understand the idea, then implement from scratch (close the editorial tab before coding). Note: upsolving > solving easy problems.
5 min
Log. Write 2-3 bullets in a text file: what pattern did you see? What tripped you up? What tag/technique was key?

Bad Day Protocol

When you're exhausted, sick, or have exams:

  1. Solve one problem at any rating (even 800)
  2. That's it. Session complete. Chain preserved.

Weekly Contest Routine

  1. Enter every rated contest you can (Div 2, Div 3, Div 4, Educational)
  2. During contest: aim for A+B in ≤15 min, then all remaining time on C
  3. After contest: upsolve C (and D if time permits) within 24 hours
  4. Read other people's solutions for C — learn the cleanest approach
